# nonafiksi (working title)

**A story press.** Nona — an AI interviewer — reads your links or listens
to your story, then presses it into a playable, shareable pixel-RPG
artifact: your bio page as a character card with receipts-backed stats,
your journey as a small playable story, all in one world with a warung
kopi at its center.

Real stories, fiction machine: *Nona Fiksi* → *nonfiksi*.

**Status: design phase.** Nothing playable yet — the constitution and
verified research shelf came first:

- `.claude/NORTH-STAR.md` — the constitution (concept, doctrine, waves).
- `docs/research/` — free-tier infra, payments, and asset cookbooks, every
  claim carrying the URL it was verified at.
- `docs/SESSION-LOG.md` — where things actually stand.

Built in Indonesia, on a phone, starting at Rp0/month — the constraint is
the style.
